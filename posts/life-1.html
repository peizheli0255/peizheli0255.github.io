把个人网站当作一个长期的房间

这里是一篇博客文章模板。你可以把生活记录写成文章，把图片放在 assets/posts 文件夹里，再从首页链接过来。

如果以后文章很多，推荐升级到 Hugo、Astro 或 Jekyll，用 Markdown 写文章，再自动生成这些页面。

This is a blog post template. You can write life notes as articles, put images in assets/posts, and link them from the homepage.

If there are many posts later, Hugo, Astro, or Jekyll would let you write in Markdown and generate these pages automatically.
