科研

Determining Optimal Nitrogen Concentration Intervals Throughout Lettuce Growth Using Fluorescence Parameters

Peizhe Li1, Zhangtong Sun1, Yongxia Yang, Miao Lu, Huimin Li, Haoran Yan, Jin Hu*, Yuling Song*

Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, 226 (2024) 109438.

1 Equal contribution. * Corresponding authors.

本研究基于荧光参数确定生菜不同生长阶段的最优氮浓度区间，为设施农业中的营养管理提供参考。

This paper uses fluorescence parameters to determine optimal nitrogen concentration intervals across lettuce growth stages, providing a reference for nutrient management in controlled agriculture.
